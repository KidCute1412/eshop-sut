## Probe Question Answers

- Clarity: The participant stated that the password requirements were unclear because the form indicated that a special character was required, but common special characters such as @ and ! were rejected. The participant was also briefly confused after registration because the Login page still displayed the "Register" heading.
- Error recovery: The participant reported that they repeatedly tried different passwords and later removed the phone number entirely to resolve the validation error. However, because the error message remained even after removing the value, they could not determine how to recover from the error.
- Speed: The participant felt that the registration and login process was reasonably quick, but updating the profile took much longer than expected because repeated validation errors prevented task completion.
- Trust: The participant reported low confidence in the profile update process because the system repeatedly displayed validation errors for a seemingly valid phone number. As a result, the participant was unsure whether any information could be saved correctly.
- Additional: The participant commented that inconsistent labels and validation messages reduced confidence in the system and made it difficult to determine whether they were on the correct page or whether the application was functioning correctly.

## Facilitator Summary

- Password validation rules did not match the guidance displayed on the Register page.
- Login page displayed an incorrect "Register" heading after successful registration.
- Phone number validation rejected a valid value and continued rejecting even after the field was cleared.
- Validation errors significantly reduced participant confidence in the system.
