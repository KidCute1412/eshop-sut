## Recruitment script (for your own reference / to send to candidates)

> "Hi [Name], I'm doing a school assignment testing the usability of a demo shopping website's "forgot password" feature. It would take about 5 minutes of your time — you'd try to reset a password on a test site while I record your screen and take notes (no real account, no real data). Would you be willing to help? I'd need to note down your name and a way to reach you (e.g. Zalo) in case my instructor wants to confirm you're a real participant."

## Session outcome notes

- All 4 sessions were screen-recorded (see `../videos/`: `long.mp4`, `linh.mp4`, `tien.mp4`, `khai.mp4`).
- No scenario/script changes were needed after P1 — the flow (forgot password → email → OTP → new password) was unambiguous to read aloud and every participant understood the goal immediately. The friction that emerged was entirely at the final "new password" step (see Phase 2/3), not with understanding the instructions.
